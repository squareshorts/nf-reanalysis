# Protocol summary

This summary records the protocol details used in the revised manuscript. It is based on Sections 2.1.1 and 2.1.5, Figure 18, and Table 5 of the December 2020 *Análise do Protocolo de Treinamento Cognitivo com Neurofeedback* report.

## Training task and feedback

Training used BrainAvatar with a BrainMaster headset. The participant controlled the position of a spaceship and attempted to maintain it at a central target. A visual indication and an auditory beep signaled that the training criteria had been met. The reinforcement criteria were simultaneous increase in SMR power (12--15 Hz), reduction in theta power (4--7 Hz), and reduction in high-beta power (20--30 Hz).

The protocol report states that reward and inhibition thresholds were set automatically by the software. A score was registered when the three EEG criteria were maintained for at least 500 ms. The report also describes possible individual adjustments to the temporal persistence criterion in increments of approximately 0.25--0.75 s and to the SMR frequency range within 12--15 Hz in steps of approximately 1 Hz. Participant-specific threshold histories were unavailable; the archive does not claim that a particular adjustment occurred for every participant.

## Session schedule

The detailed schedule describes four training days, with two 40-minute sessions per day, one in the morning and one in the afternoon, separated by approximately four hours. Figure 18 and Table 5 report eight session scores for eight trained operators. A sentence at the end of Section 2.1.5 states that the protocol totaled four sessions; this conflicts with the detailed schedule and the eight-session record. The revised manuscript uses eight sessions over four training days, supported by the detailed schedule and session series.

## Comparator

The control group did not receive contingent neurofeedback. No sham or yoked feedback was provided.

## Resting EEG features used in this reanalysis

The derived table contains eyes-open pre/post resting EEG features. The manuscript reports a 19-channel 10--20 montage sampled at 256 Hz, preprocessing with 0.5--45 Hz bandpass and mains notch filtering, visual artifact rejection, ICA-based ocular artifact correction, and common-average rereferencing. C3 and C4 features were averaged within participant and time point before the main analyses.

Welch spectral estimates used 512-sample Hann windows (2 s) with 256-sample overlap (50%). PAC used delta phase (0.5--4 Hz) and SMR amplitude (12--15 Hz), 18 phase bins, 200 circular-shift surrogates, a minimum shift of 5 s, and 2-s edge trimming. The PAC measure is a surrogate-normalized modulation-index z-score.
